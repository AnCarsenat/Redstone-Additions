The guidelines live in the docs, next to the rest of the developer documentation:

- [Getting Started](docs/getting-started.md) — building, strict mode, comparing against a reference pack.
- [Architecture](docs/architecture.md) and [Project Reference](docs/project-reference.md) — how the

- [Release and Versioning](docs/release.md) — bumping the version (`version` in
  `redstone_additions/gradle.properties`) and publishing.
